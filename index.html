1.0  INVESTMENT DESCRIPTION
1.1Corporate Entity Zhipu AI — Beijing Zhipu Huazhang Technology Co., Ltd.
1.2Principal Business GLM frontier-model family; MaaS platform delivering API, agent and coding intelligence to B2B customers
1.3Investors Outside investors and its affiliates, associates and other investors collectively hereinafter referred to as the “Series B lead investor and affiliates”.
1.4Financing Required US$342M as common equity (primary) with terms and conditions as described below; single closing at completion.
1.5Use of Proceeds The net proceeds from the financing shall be applied towards compute capital expenditure and frontier research and development, as agreed between the Investors and the Company.
1.6Capital & Investment Structure US$1.30bn post-money; Investors hold 26.3% fully diluted. Investors support a future ESOP pool up to 10%, sized with board approval (investor director included).
1.7Restrictions on IPO The Company may list at any time with the specific approval of 75% of its shareholders plus board consent, which includes the investor director (see 2.4).
2.0  SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT
2.1Conditions Precedent
  • 2.1.1Board and existing-shareholder approvals; business, legal & financial DD satisfactory to the Investors
  • 2.1.2Regulatory: foreign-investment security review and AI-sector clearances
  • 2.1.3Key-man life insurance; founder employment & IP agreements executed within 30 days of definitive agreement
  • 2.1.4SHA and articles in agreed form; Investor IC approval; agreed budget completed
2.2Termination The SHA remains in effect while the Investors hold any equity, or until a Liquidity Event: (a) a firm underwritten public offering; or (b) any issuance, transfer or encumbrance giving control, or a transfer of substantial assets, to a third party.
2.3Reporting Provisions Until a Liquidity Event, and not conditioned on holding level:
  • 2.3.1Quarterly management accounts + ARR / token-volume / gross-margin KPI pack vs budget
  • 2.3.2Annual audited HKFRS financials; capex budgets; agendas & minutes of board meetings
  • 2.3.3Board convenes on Investor request; any matter tabled on demand
2.4Board Representation — tiered by stake So long as the Investors hold not less than twenty per cent (20%) of the issued share capital of the Company on a fully diluted basis, the Investors shall be entitled to nominate one (1) director to the board of directors of five (5) members. Where the Investors’ holding is less than twenty per cent (20%) but not less than ten per cent (10%), the Investors shall be entitled to designate one (1) observer to attend meetings of the board of directors, without voting rights. Where the Investors’ holding falls below ten per cent (10%), the Investors shall cease to be entitled to board representation and shall retain only the information rights set out in Clause 2.3.
  • 2.4.1Applies symmetrically to every shareholder — including strategic anchors
  • 2.4.2Veto rights & reporting do not step down with dilution; seats sunset at listing per HKEX INED rules
2.5Shareholder Support The Investors give no warranty or guarantee for facilities required by the Company in the ordinary course of business.
2.6Right of First Offer Prior to any Liquidity Event, a transferring holder offers to all holders pro rata; 30-day acceptance window. Family / affiliate transfers exempt.
2.7Pre-emptive Rights On any new issuance the Investors may subscribe so as to maintain their percentage.
2.8Tag-along Rights If the founders sell and together retain < 50%, Investors may sell all shares on the same terms, in priority; otherwise proportional participation applies.
2.9Exit
  • 2.9.1Investors have the right, but not the obligation, to sell at IPO; Company to use best endeavors for advantageous listing timing
  • 2.9.2Redemption: no investor put before year 7; thereafter only if no liquidity event, at cost, with board consent — exit remains listing or trade sale, not a forced buyback
2.10Listing The Company shall procure listing on HKEX Main Board.
2.11IPO Lock-up Each shareholder — including the Investors and founders — shall not sell, transfer or encumber any shares for 180 days following listing. Early release or shortening lies with the Company and the underwriters, so the Company keeps flexibility to time follow-on liquidity to market conditions.
3.0  OTHERS
3.1Expenses (binding) All transaction expenses, including legal and commercial DD, borne by each party respectively.
3.2Governing Law Subscription and Shareholders Agreement governed by the laws of Hong Kong.
3.3Arbitration Disputes referred to HKIAC arbitration in Hong Kong, mutually agreed arbitrator.
3.4Exclusivity (binding) 30-day exclusivity from signing; no solicitation or competing discussions.
3.5Definitive Documentation This term sheet is non-binding except 3.1 (Expenses) and 3.4 (Exclusivity); consummation subject to definitive agreements and CPs.
3.6Confidentiality The Investors keep all due-diligence and data-room information confidential, use it solely to evaluate this transaction, and return or destroy it if the deal does not proceed. Data-room access is tiered and watermarked.
3.7Non-solicit & Non-circumvention For 24 months from signing, the Investors shall not solicit the Company’s employees, nor circumvent the Company to transact with its partners (cloud providers, customers) introduced during diligence.
3.8Announcements Neither party discloses the existence or terms of this transaction without the other’s prior written consent, except as required by law or regulators.
3.9Notices Notices under this term sheet and the definitive agreements shall be in writing, delivered by email or courier to the addresses on the signature pages, and deemed received upon delivery.
3.10Amendment & Waiver This term sheet may be amended or waived only in writing signed by the Company and the Investors; binding clauses may only be waived by the party entitled to their benefit.
3.11Entire Agreement This term sheet supersedes all prior discussions between the parties on its subject matter and records the complete proposal as of the date above; definitive agreements will supersede it upon execution.
