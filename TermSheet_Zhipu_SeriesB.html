1.0  INVESTMENT DESCRIPTION
1.1Corporate Entity Zhipu AI — Beijing Zhipu Huazhang Technology Co., Ltd.
1.2Principal Business GLM frontier-model family; MaaS platform delivering API, agent and coding intelligence to B2B customers
1.3Investors Outside investors and its affiliates, associates and other investors collectively hereinafter referred to as the “Series B lead investor and affiliates”.
1.4Financing Required US$342M as common equity (primary) with terms and conditions as described below; single closing at completion. Funds utilization: compute capex and frontier R&D, agreed between the Investors and the Company.
1.5Capital & Investment Structure US$1.30bn post-money; Investors hold 26.3% fully diluted; existing strategic shareholders (Alibaba / Tencent / Meituan / Ant) remain on the register. Investors support a future ESOP pool up to 10%, sized with board approval (investor director included).
1.7Restrictions on IPO The Company may list at any time with the specific approval of 75% of its shareholders plus board consent, which includes the investor director (see 2.5).
2.0  SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT
2.1Conditions Precedent
  • Board and existing-shareholder approvals; business, legal & financial DD satisfactory to the Investors
  • Regulatory: foreign-investment security review and AI-sector clearances
  • Key-man life insurance; founder employment & IP agreements executed within 30 days of definitive agreement
  • SHA and articles in agreed form; Investor IC approval; agreed budget completed
2.2Termination The SHA remains in effect while the Investors hold any equity, or until a Liquidity Event: (a) a firm underwritten public offering; or (b) any issuance, transfer or encumbrance giving control, or a transfer of substantial assets, to a third party.
2.4Reporting Provisions Until a Liquidity Event, and not conditioned on holding level:
  • Quarterly management accounts + ARR / token-volume / gross-margin KPI pack vs budget
  • Annual audited HKFRS financials; capex budgets; agendas & minutes of board meetings
  • Board convenes on Investor request; any matter tabled on demand
2.5Board Representation — tiered by stake
≥ 20% holding → 1 of 5 board seats  ·  10–20% → board observer  ·  < 10% → information rights
  • Applies symmetrically to every shareholder — including strategic anchors
  • Veto rights & reporting do not step down with dilution; seats sunset at listing per HKEX INED rules
2.6Shareholder Support The Investors give no warranty or guarantee for facilities required by the Company in the ordinary course of business.
2.7Right of First Offer Prior to any Liquidity Event, a transferring holder offers to all holders pro rata; 30-day acceptance window. Family / affiliate transfers exempt.
2.8Pre-emptive Rights On any new issuance the Investors may subscribe so as to maintain their percentage — the guard that keeps the ≥20% board-seat threshold intact through the pre-IPO round.
2.9Tag-along Rights If the founders sell and together retain < 50%, Investors may sell all shares on the same terms, in priority; otherwise proportional participation applies.
2.10Exit
  • Investors have the right, but not the obligation, to sell at IPO; Company to use best endeavors for advantageous listing timing
  • Redemption: no investor put before year 7; thereafter only if no liquidity event, at cost, with board consent — exit remains listing or trade sale, not a forced buyback
2.11Listing The Company shall procure listing on HKEX Main Board.
2.12Expenses (binding) All transaction expenses, including legal and commercial DD, borne by each party respectively.
2.13Governing Law Subscription and Shareholders Agreement governed by the laws of Hong Kong.
2.14Arbitration Disputes referred to HKIAC arbitration in Hong Kong, mutually agreed arbitrator.
2.15Exclusivity (binding) 30-day exclusivity from signing; no solicitation or competing discussions.
2.16Definitive Documentation This term sheet is non-binding except 2.12 (Expenses) and 2.15 (Exclusivity); consummation subject to definitive agreements and CPs.
2.17Confidentiality The Investors keep all due-diligence and data-room information confidential, use it solely to evaluate this transaction, and return or destroy it if the deal does not proceed. Data-room access is tiered and watermarked.
2.18Non-solicit & Non-circumvention For 24 months from signing, the Investors shall not solicit the Company’s employees, nor circumvent the Company to transact with its partners (cloud providers, customers) introduced during diligence.
2.19Announcements Neither party discloses the existence or terms of this transaction without the other’s prior written consent, except as required by law or regulators.
